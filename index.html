What Temlas Properties does

End-to-end property income management — from finding the deal to running it day to day.

MOST POPULAR 🏠

SA Management

Full serviced accommodation management on Airbnb and Booking.com. Listing setup, dynamic pricing, guest messaging, check-in/out, cleaning coordination, and monthly owner reports. You earn. We operate.

🔍

Deal Sourcing

We find below-market-value properties with strong yield potential, run the full due diligence, and hand investors a packaged deal pack with all the numbers done.

🔁

Rent to Rent

We lease your property on a guaranteed monthly rent, convert it to a high-performing short-let, and manage everything. You receive a fixed income with zero voids and zero hassle.

🧹

Cleaning & Turnovers

Professional cleaning coordinated between every guest stay. Linen, restocking, quality checks — all handled so your listing stays 5-star ready at all times.

🔧

Maintenance Coordination

Rapid response maintenance with trusted local contractors. We handle the calls, quotes, and follow-up — protecting your asset without adding to your workload.

🎥

Remote Property Viewing

Based overseas, relocating, or too far to visit in person? We attend the property on your behalf and conduct a thorough live or recorded video walkthrough — for buyers, investors, and tenants alike. Make confident decisions without travelling, and avoid wasting time on properties that don't match the photos.

Property Consultancy
